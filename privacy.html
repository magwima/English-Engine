In short: you sign in with Google (or a personal key from your teacher) so your progress is saved and your teacher can follow it. We keep your name, email and practice. Only your teacher can see it. There are no ads, no tracking and nothing is sold. Accounts not used for 6 months are deleted, and you can download or delete everything yourself at any time.

Legal basis

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and for your teacher's legitimate interest in following his students' progress and helping them (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Who can see it

Only your teacher, in his teacher dashboard. Other students can't see your data. We share it with nobody else, except these services that make the site work:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database. Data is stored in Western Europe. Cloudflare may process data in other countries under the EU's standard contractual clauses.
  • Google handles the sign-in. Google knows that you signed in to Psotek English.
  • YouTube plays the videos of the week (in privacy-enhanced mode). YouTube may set cookies when you press play. Google Fonts provides the fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

Your account and everything in it is deleted automatically after 6 months without signing in. Sign-in cookies expire after 180 days. You can delete your account sooner at any time.

Your rights

You can see, download, correct or delete your data, and object to or restrict how it is used. In the app: ⚙ → Your account → Download my data or Delete my account (deleting is immediate and permanent). For anything else, contact your teacher. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Poland the UODO, in Romania the ANSPDCP.

Psotek English is for adult learners. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.
