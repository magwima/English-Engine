How to play
  • W/↑ gas · S/↓ brake · AD/←→ steer
  • Hold SPACE to vacuum. You suck up more balls, but you drive slower.
  • Every ball fills your boost bar. Press SHIFT (or E) to boost.
  • Boost into a rival to smash them. They drop up to 3 balls you can steal.
  • Hit ramps to fly over crates and grab the balls in the air. Gold balls are worth 5.
  • Stars: finish the race, finish 1st, and collect enough balls (50%, 45% or 40% depending on the track). Finish a track to unlock the next.
  • P or ESC pauses · M mutes · R restarts.
